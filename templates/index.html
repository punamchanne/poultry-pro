Connected Hardware Specifications

Verify actual pin mapping and calibration targets on the ESP32 motherboard.

DHT

DHT11 Temp & Humidity

Digital Pin 4

Supplies digital telemetry for coop ambient temperature and moisture. Triggers exhaust systems if air temperature limits exceed 31°C.

Model Calibration: +/- 2°C Accuracy
MQ

MQ135 Gas Sensor

Analog Pin A0

Measures ammonia gas (NH3), alcohol, benzene, and carbon dioxide concentrations. Heavy ammonia triggers direct air exhaustion alert.

Model Range: 10 - 1000 ppm
SOIL

Soil Bedding Node

Analog Pin A1

Tracks bedding moisture through wooden shavings. Excess moisture encourages disease and signals the operator to replace the wood-chips bedding immediately.

Threshold safety: < 40% moisture
SR04

HC-SR04 Ultrasonic

Pins 12 & 13

Tracks food silo height. Triggers Echo/Trigger sound pulses inside feed containers to determine depth displacement and calculates silo volume percentage.

Trigger GPIO 12, Echo GPIO 13
FAN

5V Relay Exhaust Fan

Digital Pin 14

Magnetic electromagnetic 5V relay switch linked to the exhaust system. Regulated automatically or overridden manually by the user from this portal.

Mode status: Isolated Contacts
